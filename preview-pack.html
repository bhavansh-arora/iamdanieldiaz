Preview pack · For Daniel

Your ideas, built.

Rather than a list of ideas for your coaching page, we built them into a working site, using your own story, words, photos and client results. Here's what we noticed, what we built, and how to look around.

Start on the coaching page
What we noticed

A strong story, a few leaks.

Your current site already does a lot right: a powerful story, specific client results, and a Yahoo Finance top 10 that most coaches don't have. These are the places owners slip away.

01

The best proof sits deep

Yahoo Finance, a company grown 5x in under a year and the 32-mile Grand Canyon run are spread across inner pages, where a busy owner may never scroll.

02

One long application

It asks great questions, but on one long page. On a phone that's a lot of scrolling before anyone presses send.

03

Sign-ups may not be saved

The newsletter block shows a Squarespace notice that it "needs a storage option", which usually means new subscribers aren't being kept.

04

IGNITION's link preview

When the IGNITION page is shared, its preview text still reads "built on Replit. Update this description to reflect the app."

What we built

Every idea, working.

Each piece links to the live page with notes switched on, so you can see what it does and why it's there.

01

A home page that leads with proof

Your cut-out cover, your line, 15 years and the Yahoo Finance top 10 in the first five seconds.

 02

A coaching page that qualifies

Peak Now Coaching and the VIP Day side by side, who it's for, and the questions answered before the call.

 03

The Peak Life Scorecard

Eight questions, achievement vs fulfillment, a profile and the right next step. Results can be sent to you.

 04

A five-step application

Short, tap-to-answer steps with a 1–10 commitment score. Scorecard and path-finder answers arrive pre-filled.

 05

Speaking that books

An event brief builder and an organiser kit, so every speaking lead arrives ready to quote.

 06

IGNITION, at home

The men's cohort on your main site, with a waitlist for the next cohort and proper share previews.

 07

Every story, filterable

Client stories in one place, filtered by what people came for.
