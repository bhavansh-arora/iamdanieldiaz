(03) — The fulfillment gap

After all the achievement, something was still missing.

“I identified and eliminated my limiting beliefs so that my thoughts powered my decisions, which influenced my actions. Brick by brick, I built a new fortress of powerful habits that protected my mindset. I hit my stride.

But after all the achievement, I was lacking fulfillment. I wanted to give more. I wanted a life of greater impact. Personal development helped me maximize my business, without a doubt. What I didn't know is that it would also help me discover my true calling.”
