What's the difference between 1:1 coaching and a VIP Day?{{icon:plus}}
Peak Now Coaching is a six or twelve-month partnership: deep, sustained transformation with ongoing support and accountability. The VIP Day compresses that intensity into one full, in-person day, for people who want to level up fast.
How much does it cost?{{icon:plus}}
Your investment depends on the program and length. Daniel shares it on your discovery call, once you both know it's the right fit.
I already know what to do. Why would I need a coach?{{icon:plus}}
Most high achievers do know what to do. The gap is the beliefs, energy and habits that decide whether you actually do it, consistently, when life gets loud. That's the work.
Is this only about business?{{icon:plus}}
No. Business results matter, and so do your health, relationships, mindset and self-love. Clients often come for one and end up transforming all of them.
How do I get started?{{icon:plus}}
Book a free discovery call. If you already know working with Daniel is what you need, go straight to the application.
What if I'm not sure I'm ready?{{icon:plus}}
You don't need to have it all figured out, you just need to be willing. Take the two-minute Peak Life Scorecard, or use the discovery call to find out, with no pressure either way.
